## Reading the dates

**A date here is a scheduled term expiry, not cleared rights.** `confirmed` means a copyright renewal is on record, so the full 95-year term is running and the date is solid. Anything else is the *latest possible* date — the work may already be free if its copyright was never renewed, or may be a foreign work whose US copyright the URAA restored. Package 2 confirms a specific claim; this only says when a term is scheduled to end.
