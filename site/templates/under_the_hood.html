A real bug we caught

Open Library's author-search data conflated translators, editors, and unrelated people as if they'd written the book they were merely catalogued near. The corpus briefly had Shakespeare's Titus Andronicus credited to John Muir — the naturalist, not a playwright — plus ~90 other real titles with the wrong author attached. Caught by cross-referencing an independent dataset, root-caused to the exact line of code, fixed at the source, and re-verified clean against every case found. This is what "Package 2 independently confirms" above is actually protecting against.
