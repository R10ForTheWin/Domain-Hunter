Under U.S. copyright law, protection runs through December 31st of the year an author's term ends — so works don't trickle into the public domain, they all drop on a single date: January 1st. This page will show, for each of the next 5 January 1sts, which authors' works cross that line and which titles from our corpus that actually affects.

It depends on the Forward-Looking PD Calendar package — reliable author death-year research (life + 70 years), cross-referenced against the book corpus so the output is real titles, not just names. Still in development.
